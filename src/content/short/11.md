![Boyfriend meme: Devenv.sh looking at mise](public/meme/mise_devenv.jpg)
I am slow in the hype-cycle. [Devenv.sh](https://devenv.sh/) for years was my loyal **environment manager** and, with all it quirks in fish shell in particular, was good enough. One day in which I could't get [SecretSpec](https://devenv.sh/blog/2025/07/21/announcing-secretspec-declarative-secrets-management/) to work I found out about [Mise](https://mise.jdx.dev/) which its rich ecosystem. Granted there is much more magic involved then in Devenv.sh but **features, speed and reliability** is unmatched in any way. Give it try you even can pull [packages from nixpkgs](https://github.com/jbadeau/mise-nix) which was the last straw keeping me in devenv.sh.
