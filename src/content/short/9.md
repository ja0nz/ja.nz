I only recently discovered [nix-community/comma](https://github.com/nix-community/comma) but can't stop praising it for its utility. Need a tool - comma has it! Need [ponysay](https://github.com/erkin/ponysay), but don't want to nix-rebuild or forgot how to type nix run again. How about running:

```sh
, ponysay "its that easy?"
```

Works with **sudo** and even shell **piping**
